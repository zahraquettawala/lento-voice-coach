Lento

Your privacy

Your singing stays on your phone
Lento listens to the microphone only while you sing, to measure your pitch. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
Your progress stays on your phone
Lesson scores, your song and your settings are saved in this browser only. There are no accounts, and nobody else can see them.
Anonymous counts, no ads
Lento counts things like visits, finished lessons and errors with PostHog, under a random id, so we can make it better. That never includes your singing or your name. No cookies, no ads. Like any website, the app also loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its pitch detector from jsDelivr.
Your email, only if you share it
If you leave your email with feedback, it's kept with your feedback and lesson counts in PostHog and used only to reply or send Lento news. Ask and it's deleted.

Want a fresh start? This deletes your lesson history, streak and song from this phone. It can't be undone.
